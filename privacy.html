Privacy policy

Science of Maybe Publisher · Last updated November 25, 2026

Science of Maybe Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Science of Maybe to upload, caption and schedule videos on that channel. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

What the app accesses

  • The signed-in channel's ID, title and handle, to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.
  • Permission to upload videos, thumbnails and captions, and to update a video's privacy setting and publish time.

How it is used and stored

  • The app only acts on the owner's own channel, on the owner's own computer.
  • The sign-in token is stored only in a protected file on that computer. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • The app does not collect, sell or share data about viewers or anyone else, and does not use cookies or analytics.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Deleting the token file on the computer also removes the stored data.
